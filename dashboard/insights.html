About these AI insights

HOW AI ANALYSIS WORKS

A simple explanation

The AI reads the words in each request, groups similar concerns, and identifies the tone as concerned, neutral, or positive.

It does not know who wrote a request, and it cannot decide what the board should do. Every result needs a human review.
