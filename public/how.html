Talk like a person

Start by typing or speaking. There are no menus or categories to pick and no need to know which office handles what.

It fills in what it can, then asks

Anything it can tell from your words goes straight into the form. Whatever's still missing, like the exact spot or how the board can reach you, is clearly marked. Change anything you like.

Answer however you like

Fill in the gaps any way that's easiest. Keep writing and the form keeps up, say it out loud, or tap a box and type. You can switch from one to another whenever you want.

Routed, with history attached

It goes to the committee that hears that topic, and to the city agency too when one handles it. The board's recent resolutions and votes on the same issue show up for you and travel with your comment.

For neighbors

  • One place to speak up, in your own words.
  • You're asked only for what's actually missing, and you can answer any way you like.
  • You see right away that the board has dealt with this before, and what it decided.

For board members

  • Comments arrive complete, already sorted by committee, with a clear location and a way to follow up.
  • Each one comes with the past resolutions it relates to.
  • Less time redirecting email and more time on the agenda.
Tell Us is a civic tech demo for Manhattan Community Board 2. It is not an official NYC service. Example resolutions are from CB2 Full Board minutes, April and July 2026.
