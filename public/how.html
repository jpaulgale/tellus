Say it in your own words. We'll find the form and fill it.

No more hunting for the right place to be heard. Tell Us picks the form from what you write, whether that's the community board's own intake or a city form like a 311 service request, fills in what it can, asks for anything required it couldn't, and sends it where it needs to go.

Speaking up shouldn't take research.

Today, a neighbor with a problem has to google their way to the right portal, form or 311 category before they can be heard.

Today

  • Which community board covers this area?
  • Which of the board's 12+ committees handles this?
  • Is it even a community board issue, or is it 311, DSNY, NYPD?
  • OK, I know who this should go to, but which form or email do I send it to, and what information do they require?

With Tell Us

Just say what's going on.

Tell Us decides who and where, extracts all of the info it can from your input, and asks for any required information you missed, in real time.

The work your board has already done, front and center.

When a message touches something your board already decided, Tell Us shows the resident that decision as they write and attaches it to the submission for your committee.

“Caudalie on 130 Greene St plays music past midnight every weekend. It's too loud.”

The complaint is about loud music, so Tell Us brings up the stipulation about music.

Each message reaches the people with the power to act on it.

Not everything is the board's to handle. Tell Us checks the topic and the address, then picks the form and the destination. The board decides which fields its own form asks for.

A resident writes“Washington Square Park should stay open later at night in the summer.”
The board's formCommunity Board 2, Parks & Waterfront committeeOffers to add a name and email so the board can reply.

Better for both sides.

For residents

  • No hunting for the right email, committee or city form.
  • Asked only for the details that make the message actionable.
  • Sure it reached the people who can do something about it.
  • Shown the board's past resolutions and minutes about the same address, so they know when it's already being worked on.

For boards and their tech partners

  • Intake arrives complete, on the board's own form, sorted to a committee.
  • 311 issues and other districts' mail go where they belong instead of the board's inbox.
  • Every submission is a structured record you can store, forward or analyze.
  • Records are ready for a dashboard where the district manager and committee members can filter and share them.
